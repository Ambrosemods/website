I'm Jonathan Ambrose — a soft robotics PhD and the CTO & co-founder of [Seamless XR](https://seamless.sg). We build AI-guided sensory diagnostics so clinicians can measure what patients can feel, and so people can get that feeling back.

## Path

I trained as a biomedical engineer at NUS, then stayed for a PhD in Raye Yeow's Evolution Innovation Lab. The work was hardware: multilayer pneumatic actuators, wearable haptics, robots you can reconfigure. That became papers, a family of patents, and then a company.

Ajinkya Bhat and I met in that lab. Seamless XR started as XR haptics; in 2024 we turned toward a gap we kept hearing from occupational therapists — sensory assessment that is slow, inconsistent, and paper-heavy. [Artemis](https://seamless.sg) is what we built instead: point-of-care testing, less paperwork, a record you can actually follow.

## Now

Most days I'm shipping product in healthcare. This site is the quieter half: [research](/research/), notes in the [blog](/blog/), and how to [reach me](/contact/).

Outside work I travel and tinker with hardware. That still informs how I think. It just isn't the main story here.
