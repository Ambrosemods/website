I'm Jonathan Ambrose — soft robotics PhD (NUS) and CTO & co-founder of [Seamless XR](https://seamless.sg), where we build AI-guided sensory diagnostics for healthcare.

My research focused on soft actuators, haptics, and wearable systems. That work spun into patents and, eventually, a company. Outside the lab and the startup, I travel and design things on a 3D printer — often for the trip itself.

This site is the overlap of those worlds:

- **Travel** — trip writeups and gear that earned its place in a pack
- **Prints** — designs you can download and make
- **Building** — occasional notes on soft robotics and healthcare tech
