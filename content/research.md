PhD in soft robotics at the National University of Singapore (Evolution Innovation Lab, Prof. Chen-Hua Yeow). Work on **multilayer pneumatic actuators**, **wearable haptics**, and **reconfigurable soft robots** — hardware that later spun into patents and [Seamless XR](https://seamless.sg).

Full profile: [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Jonathan_Ambrose5) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=zNqUuZ8AAAAJ)
